MEDIA · 2 PHÚT

Why do people still buy printed books?

Đọc bài & luyện ý ↗

A printed book offers more than information. A reader may enjoy the feel of its pages, write notes in the margins, or keep it as a reminder of a particular time. Digital books, by contrast, are easy to carry and can be searched quickly. The choice therefore depends on what the reader values. Convenience may encourage someone to read digitally during a commute, while personal attachment may lead the same person to buy a favourite novel in print.

Thử viết — Vì sao sự tiện lợi chưa đủ để thay thế hoàn toàn sách giấy? Viết một ý chính và một ví dụ.

EDUCATION · 2 PHÚT

Practical skills or academic knowledge?

Đọc bài & luyện ý ↗

A university course can connect theory with practice. Students might first study why a bridge supports weight, then test a small model in a laboratory. The theory helps them explain a result, while the experiment reveals problems that a textbook example may overlook. Work placements offer another opportunity to apply knowledge. However, training for one specific job may become less useful when technology changes. A balanced education can give students both immediate skills and the ability to learn new ones.

Thử viết — Nêu một lợi ích của lý thuyết và một lợi ích của thực hành. Dùng ví dụ từ cùng một ngành học.
